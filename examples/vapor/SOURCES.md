# Sources · 任天堂 FY2025 账本 (vapor)

Every figure comes from two documents Nintendo Co., Ltd. published on 2025-05-08 for the fiscal year ended March 31, 2025
(FY2025 = April 1, 2024 – March 31, 2025):

- **A.** *Consolidated Financial Highlights* (CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS, May 8, 2025) —
  https://www.nintendo.co.jp/ir/pdf/2025/250508e.pdf
- **B.** *Financial Results Explanatory Material, Fiscal Year Ended March 2025* (page numbers are the slide numbers printed on it) —
  https://www.nintendo.co.jp/ir/pdf/2025/250508_4e.pdf

Amounts stay in Japanese yen (no currency conversion). 1 亿 = 100 million, so 百万日元 ÷ 100 = 亿日元; 1 百万台 = 100 万台.
