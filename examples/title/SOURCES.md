# Sources · Netflix 2024 (title example)

Every figure comes from one primary source: **Netflix, Inc. Q4 2024 shareholder letter** (dated January 21, 2025), filed as
Exhibit 99.1 to the Form 8-K of the same date.

- PDF (8-K with the letter attached): https://s22.q4cdn.com/959853165/files/doc_downloads/2025/Q4-24-Earnings-8-K.pdf
- Page numbers below are the letter's own printed page numbers.

| Beat | As shown | Where | Sentence / table line in the source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2.60亿 → 3.02亿 付费会员 | p.1, summary table | "Global Streaming Paid Memberships 260.28 (Q4'23) … 301.63 (Q4'24)" (millions). 301.63M shown rounded as 3.02 亿; 260.28M as 2.60 亿. Also: "We finished 2024 with 302M memberships." |
| 2 | 933 / 805 / 507 / 1891 万 净增（Q1–Q4 '24） | p.1, summary table | "Global Streaming Paid Net Additions … 9.33 (Q1'24) 8.05 (Q2'24) 5.07 (Q3'24) 18.91 (Q4'24)" (millions) |
| 3 | 4136 万 全年净增；41 道（1 道 = 100 万） | computed from p.1 | 9.33 + 8.05 + 5.07 + 18.91 = 41.36M. The letter (p.2): "coming off a year with record net additions (41M)". 41 marks = 41.36M rounded down to whole millions. |
