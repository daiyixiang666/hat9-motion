# Sources · LEGO Group 2024 (clay example)

Every figure comes from the LEGO Group's own publications for the 2024 financial year, read at the source.
Amounts are in Danish kroner as published (DKK billion = 十亿丹麦克朗; 74.3 billion is shown as 743 亿). No currency
conversion.
